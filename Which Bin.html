    How this works and what it cannot do
    • Sorting is done by Claude from your text or photo. It separates what the item is, what it is made of, and which bin it goes in.
    • No confidence percentage is shown, because an AI's own certainty number is not reliable. When it cannot tell, it asks you a question instead of guessing.
    • Safety notes for sharps, medicines, batteries, electronics, chemicals and contaminated items come from fixed text in this app, not from the AI.
    • Bin placement is general guidance. Whether something is actually recycled depends on local facilities. Verified local rules are shown only for regions that have them loaded, and are labelled.
    • Photos are analysed when you ask and are not stored. History, counts and corrections are saved in this browser only.
    • This view cannot read your location, so you pick a region yourself.

    Local rules differ by city and country. If your area labels bins differently, follow the local labels and use this as a guide to what belongs together.
